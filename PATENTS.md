# LAE patent pledge

## Pledge

> The author has not filed and will not file patent applications on the LAE format, and will not assert any patent claim against any implementation of the LAE specification.

## Terms used in the pledge

- **"The author"** means mtq0605, the natural person who is the author and
  copyright holder of LAE. The author publishes LAE under the GitHub
  account "mtq0605", which is used as a pseudonym.
- **"The LAE format"** and **"the LAE specification"** mean LAE (Layered
  Audio Extension), in every published version. This includes:
  - LAE Lossy (LAEY), specified in
    [spec/lossy/SPEC.md](spec/lossy/SPEC.md);
  - LAE Lossless (LAES), whose design document is published in
    [spec/lossless/DESIGN.md](spec/lossless/DESIGN.md). Its normative
    specification is not yet written.
- **"Any implementation"** means any implementation, complete or partial,
  made by anyone for any purpose. This includes commercial and
  closed-source implementations. It applies whether or not the
  implementation is derived from the code in this repository.

## How the pledge applies

- The pledge is made publicly, to everyone, and everyone may rely on it.
- No registration, notice or fee is required.
- The pledge is given by the author alone.

## Patent terms in the licences

- **Specification and documentation.** These are under CC BY 4.0, which
  grants no patent rights.
- **Vectors and data.** These are under CC0 1.0, which expressly does not
  waive patent rights.
- **The author's commitment.** For these texts and data, the pledge above
  is the author's patent commitment.
- **Code.** Section 3 of the Apache License 2.0 and section 11 of the GNU
  GPL v3 contain patent licences from the contributors to the code.
- **Contributions.** Contributions are accepted under the Apache License
  2.0 (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). Section 3 of that licence
  contains a patent licence from each contributor.
